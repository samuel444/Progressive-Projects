
# Presentation guide

Recommended final story:

1. Objective and architecture.
2. Historical data and volatility regime.
3. Forecast-model comparison (optional because it is expensive).
4. Market-versus-model option pricing and validation.
5. Implied-volatility and Greek checks.
6. Monte Carlo cross-check.
7. Portfolio exposure by ticker.
8. Scenario P&L distribution and worst scenario.
9. Greek P&L attribution and residual accuracy.
10. Limitations and production extensions.

Use five headline charts:
- closing prices / realised volatility;
- market midpoint vs model price;
- portfolio Greeks by ticker;
- scenario P&L distribution;
- full-revaluation vs Greek approximate P&L.
